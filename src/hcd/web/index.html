Hidden Camera Detector

Sweep a room you are staying in for lens glints and IR dots. Network and Bluetooth scans live in the CLI and Android app.

Use this only where you have a right to look

This tool is for checking a space you are staying in or have been asked to inspect — an Airbnb bedroom, a hotel room, your own office. Do not scan other people’s networks, rooms, or devices.

  • The live view stays on this phone unless you turn on “send frames to the server.”
  • The flashlight / torch works in some Android browsers and not in iOS Safari.
  • getUserMedia needs a secure context: HTTPS or localhost. If the camera is blocked on HTTP, start the server with hcd serve --https --host 0.0.0.0 and accept the self-signed certificate warning.
